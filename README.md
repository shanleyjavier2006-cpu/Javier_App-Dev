# My Favorite Movie: Barbie in A Mermaid Tale

## About the Movie

**Barbie in a Mermaid Tale** is one of my favorite animated movies. It is a *Fantasy and Adventure* story about the teenage suffered named Merliah Summers who discovers that she is half-mermaid.

Merliah travels to the underwater kingdom of Oceana to save her mother, Queen Calissa, and discoverher true identity.

## Why I Love this Movie

- I love the beautiful underwater world.
- The characters are colorful and interesting.
- The story is full of magic and adventure.
- It teaches us to believe in ourselves.
- It shows the importance of family and friendhip.

## My favorite characters

1. **Merliah Summers** - A talented surfer who discovers she is half-mermaid.
2. **Zuma** - A friendly pink dolphin who helps Merliah.
3. **Queen Callisa** - Merliah's mother and the queen of Oceana.
4. **Kayla and Xylie** - Mermaid friends who help Merliah on her journey.
